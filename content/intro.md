# Welcome to the Water Distribution System Design Book

This book is your comprehensive guide to designing Water Distribution Systems (WDS) using Python. Whether you are a student, engineer, or researcher, you will find practical resources, clear explanations, and hands-on examples to help you master the fundamentals and advanced techniques of WDS optimal design.

Developed as part of the course CE4475910 "Optimizing WDS" at [Ariel University](https://www.ariel.ac.il/), this book provides step-by-step instructions and code snippets, empowering you to apply concepts directly to real-world scenarios.

## Book Structure

This book is organized into four core chapters that build upon one another, from fundamental economic principles to a complete design example.

1.  **Chapter 2: [Classical Economic Diameter](Economic_diameter.ipynb)**  
    Learn how to determine the most cost-effective pipe diameter by balancing capital and operational costs.

2.  **Chapter 3: [Branch Network Hydraulic Analysis](branch_calculation.md)**  
    Dive into the hydraulic analysis of branched water networks, from theory to implementation.

3.  **Chapter 4: [Optimization of Branched Networks](Optimization_of_branch_networks.md)**  
    Explore the mathematical methods for optimizing pipe sizes to achieve a least-cost, hydraulically sound design.

4.  **Chapter 5: [Complete Design Example](Branch_Calculation_Example.md)**  
    Walk through a complete, hands-on example, from loading network data to performing optimization and interpreting the results.
